Who churned

Restaurant type × age

Split by restaurant type and years in loyalty

Casual-fast is half of all churn and skews mature (59% over 5 years). Casual-dining splits evenly between early failures and long-tenured brands that shrank or were acquired. C-store is the only type dominated by early churn — 4 of 5, all in 2025.

Cuisine × age

Split by cuisine type and years in loyalty

Pizza is both the largest churn cuisine (20%) and the most mature — 13 of 17 left after 5+ years, with one of the lowest redemption intensities (0.32 per active user). Early churn concentrates in American, Snack & Ice Cream, Mexican, C-store and Burgers, the cuisines with the weakest retention baselines.

How early the warning appears

All brands

% of brands with metric more than 20% below baseline, by month before churn

Signals fire in a consistent order: repeat-visit rate first (a third of brands by M-6, 78% by M-3), new signups second, then redemptions, active users and loyalty sales together in the final 2–3 months. Redemptions per active user stayed flat until M-1 — the drop is fewer active members, not weaker offers.

Under 5 years

Brands that churned within 5 years

Younger brands show trouble earlier and more broadly: 71% had a falling repeat-visit rate by M-4 and half were down on signups. New signups is a strong early flag here (32% by M-8).

Over 5 years

Brands that churned after 5+ years

Mature brands hold volume until M-2 and then drop sharply — a renewal-not-taken pattern rather than a slow fade. Warning time is about one month shorter (3 vs 4 months); baseline health did not differ by age.

Repeat-visit rate

% of brands >20% below baseline, by restaurant type

Casual-dining gives the longest warning — half flagged by M-6, 78% by M-4. Quick-serve is quiet until M-3, then jumps to 84%. C-store shows nothing until M-2.

New signups

% of brands >20% below baseline, by restaurant type

Casual-dining is the only type where signups collapse as hard as repeat visits (89% by M-1) — full-service restaurants stopped enrolling guests at the table. Casual-fast kept acquiring members but could not convert them to habit.
